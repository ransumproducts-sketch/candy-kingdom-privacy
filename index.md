# Privacy Policy for Candy Kingdom

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026
**Last updated:** 5 October 2026

Candy Kingdom ("the app", "we", "us") is a puzzle game for Android published by **New Creatives**. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

**The short version:** Candy Kingdom has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or microphone data. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and sells optional in-app purchases through Google Play, and those Google services handle some data as described below.

---

## 1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account or sign in. We do not request access to your:

- Location
- Contacts
- Camera, photos, videos or files
- Microphone
- Phone number or email address

We do not operate a backend server that receives your personal information.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To let you keep playing, the app saves the following **only on your device**:

- Game progress (levels completed, stars, best scores)
- Coins, boosters and lives
- Daily reward, streak and spin-wheel status
- Your settings (sound and haptics)
- Purchase records needed to deliver items you bought, including the "Remove Ads" entitlement and Google Play transaction identifiers

This data is not sent to us. Part of it (coins, entitlements and purchase identifiers) is kept in the Android secure keystore. Backups of app data are turned off, so this data is not copied to cloud backups. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted, and we cannot recover it.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Candy Kingdom is free and supported by ads. We use **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC, to show banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. You can remove ads with an in-app purchase.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

- Your **Android Advertising ID**
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size)
- App activity related to ads (ad impressions, clicks, and whether you watched a rewarded ad)

Google uses this to serve ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see an ad, and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. We do not receive your Advertising ID or other personal information from this process.

Learn more:
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Your advertising choices

- **Consent.** Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. If you do not consent, ads are not requested.
- **Change your mind.** If a privacy options form is available for your region, you can reopen it in the app under **Settings → Privacy choices (ads)**.
- **Android settings.** You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's settings (typically **Settings → Privacy → Ads**). You can also delete your Advertising ID where your Android version allows it.

## 4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (coin packs, the starter bundle and Remove Ads) are processed by **Google Play**. Payments are handled entirely by Google. We never see or store your card number or other payment details. We receive only the purchase confirmation and a transaction identifier needed to deliver your item and restore Remove Ads when you tap "Restore purchases". Google's handling of your payment data is covered by the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Permissions

The app uses only the permissions it needs:

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Internet and network state | To load and show ads and to complete purchases |
| Advertising ID (AD_ID) | Used by the ads service as described in section 3 |
| Billing | To process in-app purchases through Google Play |
| Vibrate | To provide haptic feedback if you have it turned on |

## 6. Children's privacy

Candy Kingdom is intended for a **general audience and is not directed to children under 13** (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at the address below and we will help.

## 7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third parties involved are the service providers described above (Google AdMob and Google Play). We do not share information with anyone else, because we do not collect it.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Because your game data lives only on your device, you control it. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Data held by Google (such as advertising and purchase records) is governed by Google's policies, and you can manage it through your Google Account.

## 9. Security

We keep sensitive in-app data (such as your coins and purchase entitlements) in Android's secure keystore and turn off app data backups. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA/CPRA in California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Since we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are answered by the controls in this policy: the in-app privacy choices, your device's ads settings, and clearing app data. For data held by Google, please use your Google Account controls. You can also contact us at any time with questions.

## 11. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Such transfers are governed by Google's own safeguards and policies.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app adds new features or services. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Significant changes will be reflected in the app or on its store listing.
